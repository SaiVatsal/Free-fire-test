# 🎮 FREE FIRE: SURVIVAL 4K // BATTLE ROYALE

A full-featured 3D Battle Royale web game inspired by **Garena Free Fire**, featuring **4K Ultra HD graphics**, third-person shooting mechanics, smart AI combatants, shrinking safe zone, authentic HUD, procedural Web Audio, and the iconic **BOOYAH!** victory screen.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 🖥️ 4K Ultra HD Graphics & Aesthetics
- **Supersampling & High-DPI Support**: Native 4K rendering toggle (`4K Ultra HD`, `2K High`, `1080p Standard`) with crisp vector-like HUD scaling.
- **Dynamic Lighting & Soft Shadows**: 4096×4096 resolution cascaded PCF soft shadow maps.
- **Cinematic Post-Processing**: ACESFilmic tone mapping, distance atmospheric fog, glowing neon cyber accents.
- **Procedural PBR Textures**: High-resolution textures for grass terrain, concrete hangars, asphalt runways, and corrugated shipping containers.
- **Visual Combat FX**: Dynamic muzzle flash lighting, glowing 3D bullet tracer rounds, flesh and spark impact debris, and floating 3D critical damage numbers.

### 🔫 Weapons & Arsenal
- **AK-47**: High-damage full-auto Assault Rifle with realistic recoil pattern and muzzle flash.
- **AWM Sniper Rifle**: One-shot high-caliber sniper rifle with illuminated mil-dot optical scope (Right-Click ADS) and booming sub-bass sound.
- **MP40**: High-rate-of-fire close-quarters Submachine Gun with lightning-fast handling.
- **Bullet Tracers & Recoil**: Procedural recoil kick that recovers over time, tighter spread when crouching or scoping.

### 🤖 Smart AI Bots & Real-Time Battle Royale
- **9 Unique AI Combatants** (`Kelly`, `Hayato`, `Alok`, `Chrono`, `Maxim`, `Moco`, `Kla`, `Laura`, `Andrew`).
- **Tactical Combat AI**: Bots roam the island, take cover behind shipping containers and trees, strafe left/right during gunfights, fire realistic weapon bursts, and engage both the player and each other!
- **Dynamic Kill Feed**: Live notifications for eliminations across the entire island.
- **Loot Drop Crates**: Eliminated bots collapse and drop glowing loot crates containing medkits and ammo.

### 🏝️ Bermuda Island Battleground
- **Aircraft Hangar & Warehouse**: Large steel truss warehouse with concrete foundation.
- **Sniper Watchtowers**: Two-level watchtowers with sniper perches.
- **Tactical Cargo Containers**: Statically and vertically stacked colorful containers for tactical cover and flanking.
- **Iconic Airdrop**: Supply drops with towering columns of animated red smoke flares and Level 3 gold armor upgrades.
- **Shrinking Electric Danger Zone**: Pulsing blue electromagnetic energy cylinder with real-time countdown timer, warning sirens, and damage-over-time outside the barrier.

### 🔊 Procedural Web Audio Engine (Zero External Audio Files)
- Deep weapon blasts, metallic bolt clicks, reload sequences, footsteps on terrain, medkit healing chimes, zone alarm sirens, and the iconic **BOOYAH!** fanfare.

## 🕹️ Controls

| Action | Key / Mouse |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Move** | <kbd>W</kbd> <kbd>A</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd> |
| **Look / Aim** | `Mouse Movement` |
| **Fire Weapon** | `Left Click` |
| **Aim Down Sights (ADS) / Scope** | `Right Click` (Hold) |
| **Sprint** | <kbd>Left Shift</kbd> |
| **Jump** | <kbd>Space</kbd> |
| **Crouch** | <kbd>C</kbd> (Toggles lower profile & reduced recoil) |
| **Switch Weapons** | <kbd>1</kbd> AK-47, <kbd>2</kbd> AWM, <kbd>3</kbd> MP40, or `Mouse Wheel` |
| **Reload** | <kbd>R</kbd> |
| **Use Medkit** | <kbd>E</kbd> (Heals 75 HP over 3 seconds) |
